# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Founders and operators who want a fast, low-friction way to describe their business and understand what to focus on next. They may be exploring the prototype during an investor demo, so the flow must be immediately understandable and calm under attention.

## Product Purpose

LudaVia collects a small set of business signals and turns them into a focused growth snapshot. The current prototype should make the path from first impression to completed business profile feel deliberate, credible, and easy to follow.

## Brand Personality

Clear, composed, quietly optimistic. The interface should feel like a considered product tool rather than a dense intake form or a marketing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered wizard chrome, unexplained context fields, dead navigation controls, purple-heavy dashboard layouts, generic SaaS card grids, and decorative motion that competes with the task.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action obvious without explaining the interface.
- Ask for only the information needed to create a useful first snapshot.
- Use atmosphere for orientation, not decoration for its own sake.
- Keep one visual idea memorable and let the rest stay quiet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast in dark and light modes, useful inline validation, touch-friendly controls, and a reduced-motion presentation that preserves state feedback without spatial animation.
